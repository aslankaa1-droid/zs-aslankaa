About

Making land protection accessible

Zemelny Service is a Russian RegTech project that helps owners of land plots navigate the complexities of Federal Law 101-FZ on Agricultural Land Turnover and other land laws. Without legalese. With Russian technologies. Compliant with the laws of the Russian Federation.

Mission

Every landowner should understand what is happening with their plot and what to do about it

Before us, the only route for an owner who had received a notice from Rosselkhoznadzor was to hire a lawyer for 50–200 thousand roubles to analyse the situation, and roughly the same again for case support. We are changing that.

The service makes the initial diagnosis and the preparation of basic documents accessible to everyone. Only the complex cases that genuinely call for a lawyer are routed to a lawyer. This is a better outcome both for users (faster and cheaper) and for lawyers (focus on complex matters where they create the most value).

Principles

How we work

1. An app, not a corporation

We build a product, not a headcount. The team is small and focused. No unnecessary policies, corporate agreements or job descriptions beyond what is needed.

2. AI-augmented, not AI-replaced

Artificial intelligence is an assistant, not a substitute. All procedural decisions are taken either by the owner or by an accredited lawyer with a QES. The AI makes the first step fast.

3. Compliance first

When "ship faster" conflicts with "stay compliant", compliance wins. This is not a checkbox but an architectural choice from the very beginning.

4. Russian stack

YandexGPT 5.1 Pro, GigaChat 2 / Ultra, Postgres Pro 17, Astra Linux SE, Yandex Cloud. No foreign providers. Ready for inclusion in the Ministry of Digital Development register.

5. Privacy by design

The minimum personal data collected. Separate consents by purpose. Hashing of sensitive data. Account deletion on request within 30 days. No covert telemetry.

6. Openness

Documentation is open (the Design Documentation pack to GOST 34 / 19 / R 56939). The sources in the RAG knowledge base are public. Constructive feedback from users and lawyers is the main engine of development.

Technology

The Russian stack of 2026

Each component has been chosen with regulation, performance and maturity in mind. With no dependence on foreign providers.

Backend

Python 3.13 + FastAPI — the core stack. A rich ecosystem for AI / RAG. Strong talent availability in Russia. Microservices in Yandex Managed Kubernetes.

Mobile

React Native 0.83+ — a single codebase for iOS and Android. Publication in RuStore (the primary channel) plus the App Store and Google Play. Native SDK from the partner bank.

Database

Postgres Pro 17 for prod IS Class 2 (FSTEC-certified). Master + sync replica. pgvector for RAG. ClickHouse for audit logs.

Cloud

Yandex Managed K8s, region ru-central1. Smart Web Security WAF. DDoS Protection. Object Storage for documents. Astra Linux SE on prod nodes.

AI / LLM

YandexGPT 5.1 Pro and GigaChat 2 / Ultra — A/B routing. Russian LLMs within the jurisdiction of the Russian Federation. Local embeddings for private content.

Security

KriptoPro SDK for lawyers' QES. TLS 1.3+ on all channels. mTLS within the cluster via Linkerd. SAST/DAST in CI.
