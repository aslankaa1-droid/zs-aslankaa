Services

The B-3 Land Reclamation from Forfeiture module under Federal Law 101-FZ

The first product of Zemelny Service. We help owners of agricultural land plots who have received a notice from Rosselkhoznadzor (Federal Service for Veterinary and Phytosanitary Surveillance) or the Ministry of Agriculture to remedy the indicators of non-use and retain ownership.

Documents and legal responses bundled into a single secure workflow.

7 strategies

We choose the optimal route for your case

AI-2 analyses the USRN extract, satellite data, the owner's history and the region. On that basis it suggests the 1–2 most workable strategies. The final choice is yours.

S-1 · Restoration

Land Use Restoration

Cultivation of the plot in line with agricultural production rules with documented evidence: ploughing, sowing, grazing. Filing a declaration with the Ministry of Agriculture. Notification to the supervisory authority.

6–12 mo · timeline 30–80K RUB/ha · cost ~60% · of cases

⭐ Recommended in all regions. In the Yaroslavl Region the 70–110K RUB/ha subsidy makes the strategy economically viable even with hired contractors.

S-2 · Lease

Lease to a Specialist Farmer

A lease agreement with a peasant farm enterprise (KFKh), an agricultural cooperative or an agribusiness. The lessee farms the land and files declarations in their own name. Registration with Rosreestr (for terms of 1 year or longer).

1–3 mo · paperwork 0 RUB · your costs + income · rent

Especially effective in the Ryazan and Moscow Regions — an active lease market.

S-3 · PUT

Permitted Use Type Change

Changing the permitted use type (PUT) within the agricultural land category — for example, from "arable land" to "fodder production". Through the local administration. A fit when actual use diverges from the entry in USRN.

3–6 mo · timeline 5–15K RUB · cost
S-4 · Category conversion

Land Category Conversion

Conversion from agricultural land into land of populated areas or industrial land via the regional government of the constituent entity of the Russian Federation. A long procedure, but, when successful, a several-fold uplift in plot value.

6–18 mo · timeline 100–500K RUB · costs

⭐ The most profitable region is the Moscow Region, especially for plots adjacent to populated areas.

S-5 · Sale

Sale to Co-owners or Agribusiness

A transaction respecting the pre-emptive right (Article 8 of 101-FZ). Notice to the constituent entity of the Russian Federation and to other co-owners. 30 days to exercise the pre-emptive right. Properly executed through a notary.

1–3 mo · transaction 2–5% · realtor fee 0.3% · notary
S-6 · Court Challenge

Administrative Court Challenge

A claim under Chapter 22 of the Code of Administrative Procedure of the Russian Federation when procedural violations by the empowered authority have been identified. The limitation period is 3 months from the date of receipt of the notice. Lawyer representation is mandatory.

4–12 mo · proceedings 50–200K RUB · lawyer 300 RUB · state duty
S-7 · Use Verification

Use Verification — proving the plot is actually used

Building the evidence base: photographs from different years, receipts for seeds and fertilisers, statements from neighbours, satellite imagery. Optionally — a forensic agronomic expert opinion.

4–8 mo · timeline 30–80K RUB · expert opinion 50–150K RUB · lawyer

None of the strategies fits?

Some cases are complex — inheritance with dozens of co-owners, specially protected territories, bank encumbrances. AI-6 will route you to a narrowly specialised lawyer who handles exactly such matters.

Guarantees

Transparent and within the law

🔒 Personal data protection

All personal data is stored in Yandex Cloud (Russian Federation jurisdiction). Passport details are stored as a SHA-256 hash with a per-user salt. The target information security class is IS Class 2 under Federal Law 152-FZ. Consents are collected separately by processing purpose. Account deletion on request — within 30 days.

⚖ Legal correctness

All document templates are reviewed by an accredited lawyer before release. The B-3 legality memorandum is approved before the first transaction. There are no "grey" workarounds — only what is directly permitted by the legislation of the Russian Federation.

📊 Diagnostics accuracy

AI-2 is calibrated against a benchmark sample of 100+ real cases. The target accuracy is at least 80%. When confidence is low, the AI emits a warning and suggests a manual review by a lawyer.

🇷🇺 Russian technology stack
