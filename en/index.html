2026 reality

Every owner of agricultural land is in the risk zone

On 1 March 2025, Federal Law 307-FZ (FZ-307) came into force, expanding the grounds for land seizure. As of 1 September 2025, Government Decree 826 (PP-826) established specific criteria for non-use. Satellite monitoring has been enshrined in legislation.

99% of land plots in violation

In the Tver Region in Q1 2026, 99% of inspected plots showed signs of non-use under 101-FZ. This is not unique: enforcement has been tightened across the entire Russian Federation.

6 months to respond

From the moment a notice to remedy is received, the owner has at least 6 months to eliminate the violation. The window has shrunk: previously it ran up to 12 months.

Regional specifics

In Kaluga the empowered authority is the Ministry of Economic Development. In the Moscow Region, regional Law 75/2004-OZ applies. Without knowledge of the procedures, it is easy to miss a deadline and lose in court.

How it works

Three steps — from anxiety to a plan

The service is built so that the first useful action takes 15 minutes. No long forms and no legalese.

Enter the cadastral number

The number, an address or a geolocation is enough. We pull a USRN extract in the background within 5 minutes — legally and through a certified provider.

Receive a risk card

AI-2 analyses the extract, satellite data and history. It shows the risk level (green / yellow / red) with the specific indicators of violation.

Act on the plan

5 to 9 steps with deadlines. Document templates have already been generated. You sign with an SMS code — Simple Electronic Signature (SES) under Federal Law 63-FZ. A lawyer is brought in if needed.

Security and law

Compliance is built into the architecture

Not a checkbox in the privacy policy. Every component of the service is designed for Russian regulation from day one.

IS Class 2 under Federal Law 152-FZ

The target information security class for personal data is IS Class 2 (Information Security Class 2 per FSTEC requirements). FSTEC certification is scheduled for M11. All data is stored within the jurisdiction of the Russian Federation (Yandex Cloud).

Federal Law 63-FZ on Electronic Signatures

SES via SMS code — for the user. Qualified Electronic Signature (QES) via KriptoPro — for the lawyer. The signing log is retained for 5 years with IP and timestamp captured.

149-FZ — ORI regime

The audit log is retained in ClickHouse for 1 year, in line with the requirements applicable to organisers of information dissemination (ORI). Ready for FSB requests.

Frequently asked

Answers to the main questions

Is this legal? Does the service face any risks under Article 170 of the Civil Code?
The service does not provide legal assistance directly — we are an information and consulting service that connects users with accredited lawyers. All procedural actions (claims, SES-signed agreements, transactions) are executed either by the owner directly or by a lawyer using a QES. The B-3 legality memorandum is approved by a specialist lawyer before the first transaction.
How much does it cost to use the service?
Basic diagnostics and access to templates are free. You pay a fixed fee per signed document, or a percentage of the rescued plot value when a lawyer is engaged. Details are on the Services page.
What do you do with my passport details?
The series and number of the passport are stored as a cryptographic hash (SHA-256 + per-user salt) — the original is not accessible even to administrators. All personal data is stored exclusively within the jurisdiction of the Russian Federation. Consent is given separately by purpose (core service / for the lawyer / marketing). You can delete your account at any moment — with a 30-day grace period followed by full personal data wipe.
What if my plot is not in one of the seven listed regions?
In the M6 pilot (October 2026) the service operates in the Tver Region. Expansion to the 7 regions is planned for M11–M12. Further expansion will follow once the IS Class 2 certificate has been obtained and the network of partner lawyers has been expanded. If your plot is outside the cluster, leave us your email and we will write to you when we arrive in your region.
